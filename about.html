About Hidayah

Our story

Hidayah means guidance. We began as a small Quran class and grew into a full school where children and adults learn the Book of Allah, the Arabic language and the academic subjects they need to succeed.

Our teachers combine strong Islamic training with classroom experience, so a student can memorise a surah in the morning and solve algebra in the afternoon.
